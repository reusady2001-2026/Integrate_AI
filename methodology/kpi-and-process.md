# KPIs / Performance Management & Process Standards

## A) KPI & performance-management frameworks

**Balanced Scorecard (BSC)** — Kaplan & Norton (HBR 1992; book 1996). Four
perspectives linked by a strategy map (cause→effect): **Financial → Customer →
Internal Process → Learning & Growth**; lower drives upper. Public/nonprofit
variant elevates Mission/Customer above Financial.

**OKRs** — Objectives & Key Results. Andy Grove/Intel; popularized by John Doerr
(*Measure What Matters*, 2018) & Google re:Work. One qualitative, ambitious
**Objective** + 3–5 measurable **Key Results**; quarterly; stretch KRs target
~0.7 attainment; decoupled from compensation.

**KPI design** — mix **leading** (predictive/influenceable: pipeline coverage,
training hours) with **lagging** (results: revenue, churn). **SMART** targets.
Every KPI needs an owner, definition/formula, data source, cadence, baseline,
target, and thresholds (🟢/🟡/🔴).

### Example metrics by function
- **Finance:** revenue growth, gross/operating margin, EBITDA, cash conversion cycle, DSO, budget variance, ROIC.
- **Sales/Marketing:** win rate, pipeline coverage, CAC, LTV, LTV:CAC, MQL→SQL, ARR/MRR, churn.
- **Operations:** OEE, cycle time, on-time delivery, defect/PPM, inventory turns, utilization.
- **HR:** voluntary turnover, time-to-fill, eNPS, absenteeism, training hours/FTE, internal mobility.
- **Customer:** NPS, CSAT, CES, retention/churn, first-contact resolution, AHT.
- **Industry families:** real estate — NOI, FFO, occupancy, cap rate · SaaS — ARR, net revenue retention, magic number · manufacturing — yield, scrap · retail — same-store sales, sell-through.

**Nonprofit / public:** use a **logic model** (Inputs→Activities→Outputs→
Outcomes→Impact); measure **outcomes** (changed conditions), not just outputs.

## B) Process / workflow / SOP standards
- **APQC PCF** — cross-industry process taxonomy: **12 categories (5 operating + 7 support)**, hierarchical (category→group→process→activity→task). Use to name/organize/benchmark processes.
- **ISO 9001:2015** — QMS built on the **process approach** + PDCA; requires "**documented information**." Use for SOP rigor (inputs, outputs, owners, controls).
- **BPMN 2.0** (OMG) — standardized process-diagram notation (pools/lanes, activities, gateways, events). Use to model as-is/to-be unambiguously.
- **SIPOC** — Suppliers, Inputs, Process, Outputs, Customers; scope/boundary map. Use *first*, before detailed flow.
- **RACI** — Responsible, Accountable, Consulted, Informed; exactly **one A** per activity.
- **COSO Internal Control** (1992; updated 2013) — 5 components (Control Environment, Risk Assessment, Control Activities, Information & Communication, Monitoring) + 17 principles. Embed controls at risk points.

## Rules
1. Cascade KPIs from strategy — every metric traces to an objective (BSC/OKR linkage).
2. Balance perspectives — never finance-only.
3. Pair each lagging outcome with at least one leading driver.
4. Make every KPI SMART with explicit formula, source, cadence, baseline, target.
5. One accountable owner per KPI (and one RACI "A" per process step).
6. Keep the set small — 5–7 per scorecard / 3–5 KRs.
7. Avoid gaming: define numerator/denominator precisely; pair quality with quantity.
8. Scope every process with SIPOC before drawing detailed flow.
9. Model flows in BPMN; document SOPs as "documented information" (trigger, inputs, steps, outputs, owner, controls, KPIs).
10. Embed COSO controls at risk points.
11. Use APQC PCF terminology so processes are comparable/benchmarkable.
12. Review on a defined cadence (OKRs quarterly; SOPs versioned and dated).
